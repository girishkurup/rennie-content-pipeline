## Mandatory disclaimer text

All product content must include this exact sentence, unmodified:
"Always read the label and follow the directions for use."

## Claims that require legal sign-off before publishing

- Any comparison to a named competitor product
- Any claim referencing a specific speed of relief (e.g. "relieves in
  60 seconds") unless backed by a cited clinical study
- Any claim of being "the number one" or "best" in a category

## Regional restriction

Content mentioning dosage information must not be published for the UK
region without a second compliance review, regardless of how minor the
dosage reference is.
