## Tone

Content should be warm, plain-spoken, and reassuring — never clinical or
alarmist. Write as if explaining something to a friend who's a little
worried, not lecturing a patient.

## Words to avoid

Avoid the words "cure," "guarantee," and "instant" in any product
description. These make unsubstantiated claims and are not permitted in
consumer-facing copy.

## Required disclaimer

Any content describing a health-related product must include the sentence:
"Always read the label and follow the directions for use." This is a
non-negotiable compliance requirement, not a style preference.

## Preferred phrasing

Prefer "fast-acting relief" over "instant relief." Prefer "may help with"
over "treats" when describing symptom relief, unless the product has
specific regulatory approval to make a treatment claim.
